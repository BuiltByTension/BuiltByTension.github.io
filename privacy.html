We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as necessary to provide services and fulfill legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data by contacting us at builtbytension@gmail.com.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

Our services are not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
